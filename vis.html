Job Postings Analysis

Seniority Distribution

The chart below shows the distribution of job postings by seniority level. Hover over the bars to see detailed counts and percentages.

Figure 1: Distribution of job postings by seniority level in 2023

Summary: This bar chart illustrates the distribution of software engineering job postings across different seniority levels in 2023. The data reveals that Senior positions dominate the job market, followed by Mid-level roles. Entry-level positions are less common, suggesting a higher demand for experienced professionals in the software engineering field. Many job postings do not specify a seniority level, which could indicate flexibility in hiring requirements.

Role Distribution

This pie chart displays the top 10 job roles in the dataset. The size of each slice represents the proportion of job postings for that role.

Figure 2: Distribution of top 10 job roles in 2023

Summary: The pie chart above shows the distribution of the top 10 job roles in software engineering for 2023. Software Engineer positions represent the largest segment, indicating they are the most commonly advertised role. Full Stack Engineers and Data Engineers also make up significant portions of the job market. This visualization helps identify which specialized roles are most in demand, providing valuable insights for job seekers and educational institutions focusing on tech career preparation.

2021 Job Locations

Figure 4: Geographic distribution of software engineering job postings across the United States in 2021

Summary: This interactive map visualizes the geographic distribution of software engineering job postings across the United States in 2021. The size and color intensity of each circle represent the number of job postings in that state. California, Texas, and New York show the highest concentration of job opportunities, reflecting the major tech hubs in these states. The visualization highlights the coastal dominance in tech employment, with fewer opportunities in the central states.
